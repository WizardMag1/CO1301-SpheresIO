“Echoes of Tomorrow” 
Genre: Story-driven Action Adventure 
Platform: PC, PlayStation, Xbox 
Target Audience: Ages 16+, players who enjoy narrative-rich, cinematic experiences (e.g., The 
Last of Us, Control). 
Estimated Playtime: 10–12 hours 
 
1. Game Overview 
Summary: 
Echoes of Tomorrow is a third-person action-adventure game set in a near-future world where 
memories can be recorded and replayed. 
Players take the role of Lyra Voss, a memory diver who uncovers hidden truths by entering 
people’s memories. 
The gameplay blends exploration, puzzle-solving, and combat with a strong narrative 
backbone. 
Core Vision: 
To create an emotionally immersive story about identity, loss, and choice — using 
memory manipulation as both a gameplay mechanic and a storytelling tool. 
 
2. Core Gameplay Loop 
1. Investigate environments to find memory fragments. 
2. Dive into memories to uncover hidden information. 
3. Alter memories to change outcomes or unlock new paths. 
4. Confront enemies and ethical dilemmas that test the player’s choices. 
Gameplay Focus: 
• Exploration and discovery 
• Environmental storytelling 
• Light combat (non-lethal and lethal options) 
• Puzzle-solving using time and memory manipulation 
3. Player Goals and Progression 
Player Goals: 
• Progress through multiple districts of a futuristic city. 
• Collect and piece together fragmented memories. 
• Solve personal and global mysteries linked to the misuse of memory tech. 
• Make narrative choices that alter relationships and endings. 
Progression System:  
Experience and unlock upgrades for Lyra’s tools — such as deeper memory dives, time-slowing 
abilities, and enhanced scanning.

4. Setting and Tone 
World: Neo-Valis — a decaying futuristic metropolis illuminated by holographic ads and filled 
with remnants of lost technology. 
Tone: Mysterious, emotional, and atmospheric — balancing sci-fi intrigue with personal drama. 
Art Direction: Realistic but stylized; dark cityscapes contrasted with bright, surreal memory 
worlds. 
 
5. Characters 
• Lyra Voss: Protagonist, skilled memory diver haunted by her own erased past. 
• Dr. Ren Imani: Mentor figure developing forbidden memory restoration tech. 
• Vex: Antagonist and ex-diver who manipulates memories for power. 
 
6. Art and Audio Direction 
• Visual Style: Blends realism with glitch-based distortion during memory dives. 
• Color Palette: Cold neon blues and purples contrasted with warm gold memories. 
• Audio: Ambient electronic soundtrack; emotional piano for key moments. 
 
7. Unique Selling Points (USP) 
• Memory diving as a core mechanic: change the past to reshape the present. 
• Emotion-driven narrative choices affecting gameplay outcomes. 
• A hybrid of puzzle, exploration, and story-driven gameplay. 
• Visually distinct “memory realms” that contrast with the physical world. 
 
8. Target Market and Competition 
Primary Audience: 
Players who enjoy emotionally driven, cinematic games with light action and choice-based 
storytelling. 
Comparable Titles: 
• Life is Strange (emotional branching narrative) 
• Control (mystery and worldbuilding) 
• Remember Me (memory manipulation concept) 
 
9. Monetization and Release Model 
• Monetization: Single-purchase premium title ($39.99). 
• DLC Potential: Optional story expansions (prequel missions, side characters). 
• Release Platforms: Steam, PlayStation Store, Xbox Store.

10. Development Notes 
Estimated Development Time: 18–24 months 
Team Composition: 
• 1 Game Director 
• 2 Designers 
• 3 Programmers 
• 2 Artists (Environment & Character) 
• 1 Writer 
• 1 Audio Designer 
11. Concept Summary 
“Echoes of Tomorrow” is a story-driven sci-fi adventure where changing memories means changing 
reality — and uncovering the truth might cost you your own past.
