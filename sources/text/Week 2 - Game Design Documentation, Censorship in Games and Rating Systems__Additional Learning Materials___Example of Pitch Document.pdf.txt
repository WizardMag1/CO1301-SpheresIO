“SkyForge: Chronicles of the Aether” 
 
1. Game Overview 
 
Title: SkyForge: Chronicles of the Aether 
Genre: Open-World Action RPG 
Platform: PC, PlayStation, Xbox 
Target Audience: Ages 15+, fans of fantasy adventure games (The Legend of Zelda, Genshin 
Impact). 
Elevator Pitch: 
Embark on an epic journey across floating islands in a shattered sky world. Players explore, battle sky 
beasts, and rebuild ancient airships to restore balance to the heavens. 
2. Core Concept 
Tagline: 
“Forge your destiny in the skies.” 
Core Gameplay: 
• Explore vast floating islands filled with secrets and ruins. 
• Engage in fast-paced aerial combat using customizable airships. 
• Gather materials to rebuild ancient forges and unlock new abilities. 
• Discover hidden lore and uncover the truth behind the shattered world. 
Unique Selling Point (USP): 
A fully explorable open sky world with real-time aerial traversal and combat, blending 
exploration and storytelling seamlessly. 
 
3. Visual Identity 
(In a real pitch, this section would include concept art or mood boards.) 
Art Style: Stylized realism — bright, painterly visuals inspired by Studio Ghibli and Sky: Children 
of the Light. 
Tone: Hopeful, adventurous, and emotionally uplifting. 
Music: Orchestral score with ethereal vocals and wind instruments. 
 
4. Market Position 
Target Market: 
• Fantasy RPG players seeking open-world exploration and light crafting systems. 
• Gamers who enjoy emotional stories and rich, colorful environments. 
Competitive Titles: 
• The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom — exploration and creativity. 
• Genshin Impact — stylized art and fantasy combat. 
--- page 2 ---
• No Man’s Sky — sense of scale and discovery. 
Market Opportunity: 
Players are drawn to freedom, creativity, and emotional storytelling, and SkyForge blends all 
three into a cohesive experience. 
 
5. Business & Production Overview 
Monetization Model: 
Premium single-purchase game ($49.99) with optional DLC expansions. 
Projected Development Time: 
24–30 months 
Estimated Team: 
• 1 Creative Director 
• 3 Designers 
• 4 Programmers 
• 3 Artists 
• 1 Writer 
• 1 Composer 
Development Tools: 
Unreal Engine 5, Blender, FMOD 
Funding Goal: 
USD $800,000 (prototype + first playable vertical slice) 
 
6. Why It Will Succeed 
• A fresh take on open-world exploration — the “sky” as a true navigable frontier. 
• Striking, emotional visuals with broad player appeal. 
• A clear and achievable scope for an indie-AA project. 
• Strong potential for community engagement and DLC expansion. 
 
7. Summary Statement 
“SkyForge: Chronicles of the Aether” offers players freedom, beauty, and emotional depth — a soaring 
adventure that blends exploration, combat, and creativity in a breathtaking sky world.
