Example 1: Tales of Emberwood 
• Game Title: Tales of the Emberwood 
• Genre: Narrative Adventure / Fantasy RPG 
• Elevator Pitch: A story-driven adventure where every choice reshapes a magical forest 
— and the creatures who call it home. 
• Core Gameplay Idea:  Explore a hand -painted fantasy world, forge alliances with 
mythical beings, and decide the fate of the Emberwood through dialogue and moral 
decisions. 
• Target Audience: Players who enjoy story -rich, choice-driven experiences (e.g., Life is 
Strange, Ori and the Blind Forest). 
• Platform: PC, Nintendo Switch 
• Key Features: 
o Branching narrative with multiple endings 
o Emotional storytelling and voice-acted dialogue 
o Environmental puzzles tied to player choices 
o Watercolor-inspired art style and ambient soundtrack

Example 2: Shadow Circuit 
• Game Title: Shadow Circuit 
• Genre: Cyberpunk Racing / Action 
• Elevator Pitch:  In a neon -drenched city where street races decide political power, 
players battle through illegal circuits using customizable hover bikes and high -tech 
sabotage tools. 
• Core Gameplay Idea:  Fast-paced, combat-infused racing with real -time weapon use, 
hacking abilities, and dynamic track hazards. 
• Target Audience:  Fans of futuristic racing and action games (e.g., Wipeout, F-Zero, 
Cyberpunk 2077). 
• Platform: PC, PlayStation, Xbox 
• Key Features: 
o High-speed races in vertical, neon-lit cityscapes 
o Bike customization with weapons, boosters, and AI upgrades 
o Dynamic weather and destructible environments 
o Single-player campaign and online multiplayer leagues
